The documents themselves

One column per benefit type, retrieved by one query, from one collection, with one index set. Every one of them was validated twice: by Zod in TypeScript, and by $jsonSchema in MongoDB. Switch the Universal Credit tabs between v1 and v2: same collection, same indexes, same dashboard, no backfill.

The contract enforced by the database

Read live from the collection options. oneOf means a document must satisfy the shared core and exactly one benefit-specific shape — flexible, but not a free-for-all.
